The idea

There’s a first place, a second place, and then there’s the one that makes a city feel alive.

Sociologist Ray Oldenburg named it. Home is your first place, work is your second, and the third place is the ground in between, where you’re neither host nor employee. You’re just yourself, among others.

The Third Place is Nashik’s answer: a single floor where a solo founder, a growing startup, a corporate team and a podcaster share the same coffee, the same energy, and the same address.

Come for a desk. Stay for the city you’re building it in.

Podcast & content studio

Nashik’s voice, on the record.

The Studio is why The Third Place isn’t just another coworking space. Acoustically treated, camera-ready, and open to members and the public. It’s where Nashik’s founders, brands and creators record the work that gets them heard.

The setup

Purpose-built at the centre of the floor, with no windows by design, for clean, controlled sound. Professional microphones, cameras, and lighting, with a layout ready for a solo recording or a full panel.

Members get preferred rates and priority slots. Not a member? Book by the hour or half-day.

Good questions

Is there a lock-in?

Flexible terms. Upgrade, downgrade, or move on as your work changes. We’ll confirm exact terms when you enquire.

Do you offer team or bulk rates?

Yes. For 5+ seats we’ll put together a plan for your team.

Can non-members use the Studio and meeting rooms?

Yes, at standard hourly rates. Members get preferred rates and priority slots.

What about GST?

18% GST applies to coworking services in India. We’ll always tell you clearly whether a quoted price includes it.

Can I visit before I decide?

Please do. Book a tour, or pick up a Day Pass and spend a day on the floor.

About

Every city needs a third place.

We kept noticing the same thing: Nashik was full of talented people working alone. Founders at home, freelancers in cafés, teams in offices that closed the door on the city around them.

There was a first place and a second place, but no third. So we built one: a single floor where Nashik’s professionals, founders, and creators could work, meet, record, and belong.

  • Community over square footageWe rent desks, but we build a place.
  • Made in Nashik, for NashikLocal by conviction, not by default.
  • Give the city a voiceWhich is why we built a studio, not just a boardroom.
  • Room for everyoneThe intern and the CEO share the same coffee machine.

The space

A full fourth floor at Canada Corner, designed as one continuous flow: private cabins along a focused corporate spine, open startup edges, a studio at the core, and a balcony to step out and breathe. Built to feel less like an office and more like the best part of the city.

Digital Nashik

Nashik is known for its vineyards. We think it’s ready to be known for what it builds. The Third Place is a small piece of infrastructure for a bigger idea: a Nashik that keeps its talent, backs its founders, and puts itself on India’s digital map.
